# THE UNWRITTEN — Official Brand Identity & Visual Guidelines

**Brand Version:** 2.0.0 (Canonical Silver & Crimson Emblem)  
**Status:** Approved & Enforced System-wide  
**Primary Brand Motto:** *WHERE SILENCE SPEAKS*

---

## 1. BRAND EMBLEM & CORE CONCEPT

The visual identity of THE UNWRITTEN centers on the official circular emblem featuring:
- **Central Iconography:** A keyhole aperture framing a cracked classical stone mask with a raised index finger in the universal gesture of silence.
- **Atmospheric Silhouette:** The gothic Research Institute manor framed against mist and pine silhouettes in the lower hemisphere.
- **Border Treatment:** A silver metallic ring with subtle hand-etched texturing.
- **Color Accent:** A restrained crimson red slash through the title and the word **S I L E N C E**.

## 4. BRAND TYPOGRAPHY RULES

1. **Title:** High-contrast classic serif (Playfair Display / Cinzel / Cormorant Garamond aesthetic).
2. **Subtitle:** Monospaced geometric uppercase with wide kerning (`letter-spacing: 0.3em`).
3. **Motto:** *WHERE SILENCE SPEAKS* in spaced silver serif.
4. **Prohibitions:**
   - Never stretch, skew, or non-uniformly scale the circular emblem.
   - Never tint the silver metallic rim into yellow/gold.
   - Never remove or recolor the crimson accent slash in "UNWRITTEN" and "SILENCE".
