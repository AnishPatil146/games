# Junior Mode — Academic Architecture Specification

## 1. Pedagogical Mission
Junior Mode transforms secondary & higher-secondary board examination preparation into a calm, focused, and distraction-free reasoning studio. Aligned with the Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education (MSBSHSE) curriculum, it provides diagnostic clarity without anxiety-inducing leaderboards or clinical mischaracterizations.

## 2. Curriculum Isolation
- **Class 11 (FYJC)**: Complete 14-subject catalogue spanning Science, Commerce, and Arts & Humanities. Total verified question bank: 1,400 items.
- **Class 12 (HSC)**: Dedicated Pre-Board & Entrance Arena with competitive benchmark mappings. Class 11 and Class 12 curricula are strictly partitioned into independent academic spaces.

## 3. The 3 Official Difficulty Tracks
1. **EASY — Learn & Understand**:
   - Focus: Foundational axioms, standard textbook definitions, direct formula substitutions.
   - Purpose: Confidence building and conceptual grounding.
2. **MEDIUM — Board Preparation**:
   - Focus: Multi-step derivations, numerical word problems, official board examination patterns.
   - Purpose: Mastery of syllabus standards for state and central board exams.
3. **HARD — Competitive Entrance Prep**:
   - Focus: High-order thinking skills (HOTS), multi-concept integration, time-pressured deduction.
   - Stream Mappings:
     - Science (Math): **JEE Main & Advanced**
     - Science (Bio): **NEET UG**
     - Commerce: **CUET UG & CA Foundation**
     - Arts & Humanities: **CUET UG & CLAT (Common Law Admission Test)**
