# Junior Mode — Syllabus Mapping & Subject Catalog

## 1. 14 Verified MSBSHSE Class 11 Subjects

### Science Stream
1. **Physics** (Code 54): Units & Measurements, Mathematical Methods, Motion in a Plane, Laws of Motion, Gravitation, Mechanical Properties of Solids, Thermal Properties of Matter, Sound, Optics, Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetism, Electromagnetic Waves & Communication System, Semiconductors.
2. **Chemistry** (Code 55): Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry, Introduction to Analytical Chemistry, Basic Analytical Techniques, Structure of Atom, Chemical Bonding, Redox Reactions, Modern Periodic Table, Elements of Groups 1 and 2, States of Matter, Adsorption and Colloids, Chemical Equilibrium, Surface Chemistry, Hydrocarbons, Chemistry in Everyday Life.
3. **Biology** (Code 56): Living World, Systematics of Living Organisms, Kingdom Plantae, Kingdom Animalia, Cell Structure and Organization, Biomolecules, Cell Division, Plant Tissues and Anatomy, Morphology of Flowering Plants, Animal Tissue, Study of Animal Type: Cockroach, Photosynthesis, Respiration and Energy Transfer, Human Nutrition, Excretion and Osmoregulation, Skeleton and Locomotion.
4. **Mathematics & Statistics (Science)** (Code 40): Angle and its Measurement, Trigonometry - I & II, Determinants and Matrices, Straight Line, Circle, Conic Sections, Measures of Dispersion, Probability, Complex Numbers, Sequences and Series, Permutations and Combinations, Mathematical Induction and Binomial Theorem, Sets and Relations, Functions, Limits, Continuity, Differentiation.

### Commerce Stream
5. **Book-Keeping & Accountancy** (Code 50): Introduction to Book Keeping and Accountancy, Meaning and Fundamentals of Double Entry Book Keeping, Journal, Ledger, Subsidiary Books, Bank Reconciliation Statement, Depreciation, Rectification of Errors, Final Accounts of a Proprietary Concern, Single Entry System.
6. **Organisation of Commerce & Management (OCM)** (Code 51): Small Scale Industry and Business, Forms of Business Organisation - I & II, Business Services, Emerging Modes of Business, Social Responsibilities of Business, Consumer Protection, Principles of Management.
7. **Economics** (Code 49): Basic Concepts in Economics, Money, Partition Values, Economy of Maharashtra, Rural Development in India, Population in India, Unemployment in India, Poverty in India, Economic Policy of India Since 1991, Economic Planning in India.
8. **Secretarial Practice (SP)** (Code 52): Secretary, Joint Stock Company, Formation of a Company, Documents Related to Formation of a Company, Members of a Company, Directors and Key Managerial Personnel, Company Meetings - I & II, Business Communication Skills, Correspondence with Directors, Correspondence with Banks, Correspondence with Statutory Authorities.
9. **Mathematics & Statistics (Commerce)** (Code 88): Sets and Relations, Functions, Complex Numbers, Sequences and Series, Locus and Straight Line, Determinants, Matrices, Continuity, Differentiation, Commercial Mathematics, Partition Values, Measures of Dispersion, Correlation, Time Series.

### Arts & Humanities Stream
10. **History** (Code 38): First Farmers, India during the period of Chalcolithic Cultures, Early Kingdoms in India, Vedic Period, Second Urbanisation in India, Religious Movements, Maurya Empire, Post-Maurya Period, Guptas and Post-Gupta Rulers, South Indian Dynasties, Medieval Deccan, Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, Art and Architecture.
11. **Geography** (Code 39): Earth Movements, Weathering and Mass Wasting, Agents of Erosion, Climate, Global Climate Change, Ocean Water, Biomes, Disasters.
12. **Political Science** (Code 37): The State, Liberty and Rights, Equality and Justice, Role of the Judiciary, Nationalism and Democracy, Administrative System in India, Public Administration, Constitutional Government in India.
13. **Psychology** (Code 48): Story of Psychology, Branches of Psychology, Self, Human Development, Healthy Mind, Stress and Coping, Mental Health and Well-being, Psychological Disorders.
14. **Sociology** (Code 47): Introduction to Sociology, Social Institutions, Social Stratification, Culture and Socialisation, Social Change, Social Problems in India.
