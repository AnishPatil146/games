# THE UNWRITTEN — Campaign Design: Solo vs Multiplayer

## 1. Solo Campaign: The Mind Experiment
- **Protagonist**: Aarav ALONE throughout the entire journey.
- **Tone**: Intense psychological isolation, existential mystery, brutalist subterranean architecture.
- **Chamber Progression**:
  1. *Chamber 01: The Wake Up* — Sub-level cellar; 4-digit brass keypad cipher (`0745`).
  2. *Chamber 02: Three Doors* — Chamber of paradox; logical elimination of oak, chalk, and iron doors (`WHITE`).
  3. *Chamber 03: The Forgotten Room* — Victorian library rotunda; sequence memory card matrix.
  4. *Chamber 04: The Interrogation Room* — Soundproof bunker; interrogation of Mr. Halden; alibi deduction (`CONFRONT_ALIBI`).
  5. *Chamber 05: The Mirror Maze* — Hall of mirrors; 2D interactive spatial labyrinth avoiding reflection traps.
  6. *Chamber 06: The Last Human* — Bio-containment facility; moral distribution dilemma with Mira (`SACRIFICE_SERUM`).
  7. *Chamber 07: The Impossible Second* — Dispatch control room; 15-second emergency power routing under crisis.
  8. *Chamber 08: The Final Truth* — Great Memory Vault; Warden's final debrief and transcription of 4 philosophical endings (The Survivor, The Mastermind, The Humanist, The Unpredictable).

## 2. Multiplayer Campaign: The Last Alliance
- **Operatives**: 2–4 players (Aarav, Aanya, Warden, Mira callsigns).
- **Tone**: High-stakes espionage, cooperative tactical deduction, inevitable betrayal.
- **Chamber Progression**:
  1. *Chamber 01: The First Lock* — Asymmetric 9x9 Sudoku grid partition with conflict glow.
  2. *Chamber 02: Count Vesper's Gambit* — Live 8x8 Chessboard puzzle (`1. Nf6+! gxf6  2. Bxf7#`).
  3. *Chamber 03: Mind Snap* — Synchronized audio/visual symbol match under 90-second countdown.
  4. *Chamber 04: The Imposter Within* — Social deduction deliberation and voting matrix.
  5. *Chamber 05: The Memory Vault* — Shared coordinate reconstruction cipher.
  6. *Chamber 06: The Human Choice* — Team triage dilemma under cascading resource drain.
  7. *Chamber 07: The Breaking Point* — High-pressure multi-terminal synchronization.
  8. *Chamber 08: The Final Betrayal* — Secret simultaneous Prisoner's Dilemma choice: Cooperate vs Betray.
